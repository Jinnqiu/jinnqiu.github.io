Jinniao Qiu

I am a Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Calgary.

My research focuses on stochastic analysis, stochastic control, game theory, and stochastic and deterministic partial differential equations. I develop probabilistic and numerical methods, with applications to optimization, games, operational research, mathematical finance, economics, and related fields.
